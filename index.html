About

What I build

I turn raw, messy data into production ML systems that teams can trust and maintain. My work spans the full lifecycle: ingestion pipelines, feature engineering, model training, interpretable output, and deployment with monitoring. I care about making systems reusable — not just notebooks that run once.

Recent work includes an end-to-end ML pipeline for Tacoma Power (SharePoint ingestion → Data Preparation + Cleaning → NLP extraction → XGBoost + SHAP → Snowflake → Tableau), a fine-tuned 7B LLM deployed on serverless GPU, and a distributed GPT-2 training setup achieving 5× throughput on A100s.

ML depth

I'm especially interested in how models actually work — not just whether they do. That means mechanistic interpretability, knowledge distillation, and rigorous evaluation: asking what signal a model is using, what behavior is being transferred, and whether results are actually reliable.

I've done RLHF evaluation work for frontier LLM datasets at DataAnnotation, and I hold a philosophy degree with distinction — which means I can spot a bad causal claim faster than most engineers, and design experiments that actually isolate what they claim to test.

Looking for

Full-time MLE, DS, or applied AI roles in the US or remote. I work best where real data problems meet production constraints — teams where rigor matters as much as speed.
